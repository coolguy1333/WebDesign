MHS IT Club Minecraft Server Rules

Respect & Behavior

  • Be respectful to all players and staff.
  • No harassment, hate speech, or toxicity.
  • Respect claims and builds.

Chat Rules

  • No spam, flooding, or excessive caps.
  • No NSFW or inappropriate content.
  • No advertising without permission.

Item Naming

  • No offensive or inappropriate item names.
  • No fake “admin items” or misleading names.
  • No impersonating staff or players.

Economy Rules

  • No scamming or deceptive trades.
  • No exploiting shop bugs or dupes.
  • Farms must not cause lag.

Cheating & Exploits

  • No hacked clients, X-ray, or unfair mods.
  • No duplication glitches or abuse of bugs.
  • TNT duping is allowed only for farms.

Builds & Lag

  • No lag machines or excessive entities.
  • No inappropriate builds.

PvP Rules

  • No combat logging.

Staff Authority

  • Listen to staff.
  • Do not argue in public chat.
  • No impersonation.

Punishments

Minor (chat, naming)
Warning → Mute → Temp Ban
Moderate (scam, minor grief)
1–7 day ban + rollback if needed
Major (cheats, dupes)
7–30 day ban or permanent
Extreme (hate speech, server damage)
Permanent ban

Final note: Use common sense. Staff have final say.
